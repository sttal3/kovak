Kovak Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026

The short version

Kovak has no account, no servers, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your phone. We never see it, and we never sell or share it.

Who we are

Kovak is made by Strahinja Šišić, Novi Sad, Serbia (“we”). You can reach us at sisicstrahinja2@gmail.com.

What Kovak stores on your phone

To work, the app saves the following, only in its own storage on your device:

  • Your habits, check-ins, logged urges and slips, notes and messages to yourself.
  • Your profile: the name you choose, focus areas, hardest time of day.
  • Settings such as reminders, theme, currency and Focus mode rules.
  • If you turn on App lock, a scrambled (hashed) version of your code and your recovery answer, never the code itself.

This information may relate to your health, such as habits you are trying to quit. It never leaves your phone unless you export it yourself.

Focus mode and permissions

Focus mode only works if you give it these Android permissions. Each one is used on your phone and nothing is sent anywhere:

  • Usage access: to see which app is open right now and how many minutes you used the apps you chose today, so it can pause them.
  • Display over other apps: to show the “paused” screen on top of an app you chose to block.
  • List of installed apps: so you can pick which apps to pause. Only your choices are saved.
  • Notifications, alarms and starting after a restart: for your reminders and to keep Focus schedules running.

Backups

When you tap Back up, Kovak writes a file to a place you pick. If you pick a cloud folder (for example Google Drive), that service stores the file under its own privacy policy. Backup files are not encrypted, so keep them private.

If Android backup is turned on in your phone settings, Android may include Kovak’s data in your phone’s own encrypted backup to your Google account. That backup belongs to you; we cannot access it.

Purchases

Kovak Plus is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment under its own privacy policy. We only receive confirmation that Plus is unlocked. We never see your card or payment details.

Links

Support links (such as helplines) open in your phone app or browser. Those services have their own policies.

Age

Kovak is meant for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Your rights and deleting your data

Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing on our side to access, correct or delete. You are in full control: Profile → Erase all data removes everything, and uninstalling the app does the same. You still have every right the law gives you (for example under the GDPR) and can contact us with any question.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date below, and mention important changes in the app.
