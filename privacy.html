Kovak Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026

The short version

Kovak has no account, no servers, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your phone. We never see it, and we never sell or share it.

The Android app does not even have permission to use the internet, so it cannot send your data anywhere.

The only thing we ever collect is your email address, and only if you join the waitlist on our website. That is explained below.

Who we are

Kovak is made by Strahinja Šišić, Novi Sad, Serbia (“we”). You can reach us at sisicstrahinja2@gmail.com.

What Kovak stores on your phone

To work, the app saves the following, only in its own storage on your device:

  • Your habits, check-ins, logged urges and slips, notes and messages to yourself.
  • Your profile: the name you choose, focus areas, hardest time of day.
  • Settings such as reminders, theme, currency and Focus mode rules.
  • If you turn on App lock, a scrambled (hashed) version of your code and your recovery answer, never the code itself.

This information may relate to your health, such as habits you are trying to quit. It never leaves your phone unless you export it yourself.

Focus mode and permissions

Focus mode only works if you give it these Android permissions. Each one is used on your phone and nothing is sent anywhere:

  • Usage access: to see which app is open right now and how many minutes you used the apps you chose today, so it can pause them.
  • Display over other apps: to show the “paused” screen on top of an app you chose to block.
  • List of installed apps: so you can pick which apps to pause. Only your choices are saved.
  • Notifications, alarms and starting after a restart: for your reminders and to keep Focus schedules running.

Backups

When you tap Back up, Kovak writes a file to a place you pick. If you pick a cloud folder (for example Google Drive), that service stores the file under its own privacy policy. Backup files are not encrypted, so keep them private.

If Android backup is turned on in your phone settings, Android may include Kovak’s data in your phone’s own encrypted backup to your Google account. That backup belongs to you; we cannot access it.

Purchases

Kovak Plus is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment under its own privacy policy. We only receive confirmation that Plus is unlocked. We never see your card or payment details.

Our website and the waitlist

This section covers our website, not the app. If you join the Kovak waitlist, we collect:

  • Your email address.
  • The date you signed up.
  • Anything else you choose to write in the form, if it has an optional field.

Why: only to tell you when Kovak launches, to invite you to test early versions, and to send a few important updates about Kovak. We do this because you asked us to (your consent).

What we never do: sell your email, share it for marketing, or add it to anything unrelated to Kovak.

Who stores it: sign-ups are stored for us by a service provider that we use to run the waitlist. It may store data on servers outside Serbia and the EU, with the safeguards the law requires. It may only use your email to provide that service to us.

How long: until you ask to be removed, or at the latest 12 months after Kovak launches, whichever comes first.

Leaving the waitlist: every email we send has a way to unsubscribe, or you can write to sisicstrahinja2@gmail.com and we will delete your email.

Visiting the website: like any website, the company that hosts it may keep basic technical logs (such as your IP address and browser type) for a short time, to keep the site running and secure. We do not use the website to show ads or track you across other sites.

Joining the waitlist is optional. You do not need to join to use Kovak.

Links

Support links (such as helplines) open in your phone app or browser. Those services have their own policies.

Age

Kovak and its waitlist are meant for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child joined the waitlist, write to us and we will delete it.

Your rights and deleting your data

In the app: we hold none of your app data, so there is nothing on our side to access, correct or delete. You are in full control: Profile → Erase all data removes everything, and uninstalling the app does the same.

On the waitlist: you can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete your email, or withdraw your consent, by writing to sisicstrahinja2@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days.

You have every right the law gives you (for example under the GDPR and Serbian data protection law), including the right to complain to your local data protection authority. In Serbia, that is the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date below, and mention important changes in the app.
