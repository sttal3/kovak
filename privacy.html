Aboko Privacy Policy

Effective 21 November 2026

The short version

Aboko has no account, no servers, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your phone. We never see it, and we never sell or share it.

The Android app does not even have permission to use the internet, so it cannot send your data anywhere.

The only personal data we ever hold is your email address, and only if you join the waitlist on our website. That is explained below.

Who we are

Aboko is made by Strahinja Šišić, Novi Sad, Serbia (“we”). You can reach us at support@aboko.app.

What Aboko stores on your phone

To work, the app saves the following, only in its own storage on your device:

  • Your habits, check-ins, logged urges and slips, notes and messages to yourself.
  • Your profile: the name you choose, focus areas, hardest time of day.
  • Settings such as reminders, theme, currency and Focus mode rules.
  • If you turn on App lock, a scrambled (hashed) version of your code and your recovery answer, never the code itself.

This information may relate to your health, such as habits you are trying to quit. It never leaves your phone unless you export it yourself.

Focus mode and permissions

Focus mode only works if you give it these Android permissions. Each one is used on your phone and nothing is sent anywhere:

  • Usage access: to see which app is open right now and how many minutes you used the apps you chose today, so it can pause them.
  • Display over other apps: to show the “paused” screen on top of an app you chose to block.
  • List of installed apps: so you can pick which apps to pause. Only your choices are saved.
  • Notifications, alarms and starting after a restart: for your reminders and to keep Focus schedules running.

Backups

When you tap Back up, Aboko writes a file to a place you pick. If you pick a cloud folder (for example Google Drive), that service stores the file under its own privacy policy. Backup files are not encrypted, so keep them private.

If Android backup is turned on in your phone settings, Android may include Aboko’s data in your phone’s own encrypted backup to your Google account. That backup belongs to you; we cannot access it.

Purchases

Aboko Plus is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment under its own privacy policy. We only receive confirmation that Plus is unlocked. We never see your card or payment details.

Our website and the waitlist

This section covers our website, not the app. If you join the waitlist, we store:

  • Your email address, as you typed it, plus a lower-case copy we use to spot duplicates.
  • The date and time you signed up.

Why: to send you a confirmation, an email when Aboko launches, and possibly one invitation to test an early version. Nothing else, and no newsletters. We do this because you asked us to (your consent), and you can withdraw it at any time.

We never sell your email, share it for marketing, or use it for anything unrelated to Aboko.

Services that handle it for us, each only to provide its service:

  • Supabase stores the waitlist.
  • Brevo sends the emails.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile checks that a person, not a bot, is signing up. To do that it looks at technical signals from your browser, such as your IP address. It does not use them to track you across sites.
  • Vercel hosts the website and, like any host, keeps short-lived technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) to keep the site running and secure.

Some of these services may process data outside Serbia and the EU. Where they do, they use the safeguards the law requires, such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

Your browser also remembers, on your own device, the emails you submitted from it, so the page can tell you if you already joined. That stays in your browser and is never sent to us.

How long we keep it: until you ask us to remove it. You can ask at any time, and we will delete it within one month.

Leaving the waitlist: reply to any email we send, or write to support@aboko.app, and we will delete your email.

The website shows no ads and uses no analytics or advertising cookies. Joining the waitlist is optional, and you do not need to join to use Aboko.

Links

Support links (such as helplines) open in your phone app or browser. Those services have their own policies.

Age

Aboko and its waitlist are meant for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child joined the waitlist, write to us and we will delete it.

Your rights and deleting your data

In the app: we hold none of your app data, so there is nothing on our side to access, correct or delete. You are in full control: Profile → Erase all data removes everything, and uninstalling the app does the same.

On the waitlist: you can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete your email, or withdraw your consent, by writing to support@aboko.app. We will reply within one month.

We make no automated decisions about you.

You have every right the law gives you, for example under the GDPR and the Serbian Law on Personal Data Protection, including the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Serbia that is the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection (poverenik.rs).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date below, and mention important changes in the app.
